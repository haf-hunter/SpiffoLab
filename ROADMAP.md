# SpiffoLab CC — Roadmap

Here is what we are working on next for **SpiffoLab CC** (Project Zomboid Build 41 & Build 42).  
Have a feature request or bug report? Open an issue anytime in our [Issue Tracker](https://github.com/haf-hunter/SpiffoLab/issues).

---

## Planned & In Progress

### 1. Enhanced In-Game Crash Protection & Reporting
- Deeper integration with ZombieBuddy crash protection so prevented in-game crashes are surfaced clearly in the Live Log and Diagnostic Report.

### 2. Dynamic "For You" Discovery & Full Workshop Search
- Expand the "For You" mod showcase from a fixed 6-mod grid to a responsive layout that fills your available screen space cleanly.
- Upgrade free-text mod search to query the entire Steam Workshop live — not just your locally downloaded mod pool.

### 3. Per-Profile Optimizer Overlays & Version Switcher
- Planned support for switching lightweight third-party optimization overlays and AOT caches cleanly per profile without leaving mixed files behind during game build switches.

## Recently Completed

- **v2.11.0 — Profile Manager Overhaul, Smart Mod Links & Metric Gradients:** Cleaner **Profile Manager** with prominent **+ New Profile**, collapsible Workshop mod packages, single-card accordion view, hard conflict activation lock, color-coded clickable dependency/conflict links, instant mod refresh on profile switch, silent pre-launch Java cleanup, full JVM tuning & external optimizer opt-out right inside the Profile Wizard, and customizable **2-color (`Low` -> `High`) Live-Metrics chart gradients** in the Custom Theme Studio.

- **v2.10.0 – v2.10.2 — Make Your Own Lab:** Full **Custom Theme Studio** inside `Settings -> General` with **5 independent color layers** (Background, Main Panels, Sub-Areas & Fields, Accent, Text), toggleable **Color Presets** (4+4 dark/light swatches) vs. interactive **HSV Color Wheel** + brightness slider, custom `#RRGGBB` hex inputs, live WCAG contrast checks, saveable custom themes, curated fonts, free **`80%–150%` scaling** (`5%` steps), 5-layer live preview, matching Windows 11 window chrome, responsive compact header dropdown, active accent borders on Help/Settings, collapsible Live Log sidebar (`Mod Rank` & `Lag Spikes`), **`MOD-RADAR`** rename, central CSS tokens synced across all views and exported HTML reports, plus **`v2.10.1`** 4-zone responsive Live Log toolbar (16:9 & 9:16), centered count badges, scale-stable thumbnails/metrics layout, Start-tab update badge fix, live metric card borders, and updated Help Center screenshots, and critical `v2.10.2` Build 42 version detection hotfix.

- **v2.9.0:** All-new **`START` Tab** command center left of `LAUNCHER` with grunge-smoke hero banner, **Continue Last Run** across isolated profiles with 50/50 30s benchmark hardfacts linked to `REPORT` and direct total mod-updates jump into Mod Manager, 8 Quick Actions, 12 curated tips with screenshots, anonymous live Project Zomboid Steam News & Wiki patch notes fetcher, configurable default startup tab & live news toggle, plus refreshed Help Center guides and screenshots across all 4 languages.

For older milestones (`v1.0.2 – v2.8.1`) and the full version-by-version history, see [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
